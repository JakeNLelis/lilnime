DRAKMOR: BLADE OF EXTINCTION

DRAGON SLAYER SWORD

Kaelen, a lone warrior fueled by vengeance, wielded Drakmor—a cursed blade forged to slay dragons.

One by one, he hunted the mighty beasts, believing their extinction would bring peace.

But with the last dragon’s fall, the world dimmed.

Magic vanished, nature withered, and Kaelen realized too late: the dragons were not the threat, but the balance.

He threw the sword into the sea, mourning a world he unknowingly destroyed.
